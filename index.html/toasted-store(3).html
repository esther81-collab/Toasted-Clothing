Size guide

All garments have a relaxed / utility fit. Between sizes? Size up for outerwear.

Tops & jackets (chest)

  • S — 88–94 cm
  • M — 95–101 cm
  • L — 102–108 cm
  • XL — 109–116 cm

Bottoms / sets (waist)

  • S — 71–76 cm
  • M — 77–84 cm
  • L — 85–92 cm
  • XL — 93–100 cm

Shipping & delivery

We deliver across South Africa and offer free collection in Pretoria.

  • Collection (Pretoria) — Free. We confirm the point and time after your order.
  • Courier — R99 flat. Free on orders of R1,500+. Usually 2–5 working days.

You get tracking by email / WhatsApp once the order is dispatched.

Returns & refunds

You have 14 days from receiving your order to request a return or exchange.

  • Items must be unused, unwashed, with tags attached.
  • Return shipping is for your account unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong size/colour.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days after we inspect the return.

Email paxlight0@gmail.com with your order number to start a return.

Privacy policy

We collect your name, email, phone and address only to fulfil orders and send drop alerts if you signed up. We do not sell your data. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information under POPIA by emailing paxlight0@gmail.com.
